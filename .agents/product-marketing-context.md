# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-06-15*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Trim Proof turns plain-English print briefs into checked PDF/X proofs with AI-assisted creative assets and deterministic prepress validation.

**What it does:** Trim Proof helps users create flyers, posters, menus, brochures, business cards, postcards, and letterhead for print. AI can help with creative direction and non-text assets, while deterministic code owns the production file: vector text, embedded fonts, trim and bleed geometry, crop marks, CMYK-oriented output, ICC profile handling, raster DPI checks, PDF/X status, and preflight reporting.

**Product category:** AI print-ready PDF generator, online prepress tool, print proof generator, PDF/X proofing software.

**Product type:** B2B and prosumer SaaS for print-file creation and prepress handoff.

**Business model:** Free account with watermarked demo proof; $12 export credit for one clean production-oriented PDF/X export; $49/month Trim Proof Pro with 15 advanced exports.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Print files often look finished on screen but fail because the hidden production structure is wrong or unclear.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic AI design and template tools prioritize visual output, not PDF boxes, PDF/X status, color workflow, vector text, or preflight evidence.
- Traditional preflight tools are powerful but assume existing files, expert users, Acrobat plugins, or production workflow knowledge.
- Static templates do not solve copy, layout, proof generation, checkout, or preflight in one flow.

**What it costs them:** Rework, printer rejections, late campaigns, manual file cleanup, extra designer time, lost confidence, and avoidable back-and-forth.

**Emotional tension:** The buyer wants speed, but they fear the file will embarrass them when the printer catches a hidden issue.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Online print-proof and PDF preflight tools. They focus on inspection or repair but often do not generate a supported print piece from a brief.

**Secondary:** Canva, Adobe Express, Design.com, Venngage, VistaCreate, and template marketplaces. They are strong at creative starts and templates but weaker as a deterministic prepress handoff.

**Indirect:** Acrobat Preflight, Enfocus PitStop, callas pdfToolbox, FlightCheck, and print-shop production teams. These tools and teams are strong for experts but heavier than a self-serve guided proof path.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- AI creative upstream, deterministic prepress downstream.
- Supported product profiles for common print jobs.
- Vector text and embedded fonts in the production layer.
- Visible trim, bleed, safe-area, crop-mark, DPI, color, and PDF/X checks.
- Paid export path tied to Stripe and account-level usage.
- SEO library mapped to real print-search demand.

**How we do it differently:** Trim Proof separates the fun creative step from the file-validity step. The final proof is built by code with explicit print geometry and checks instead of relying on an image model to produce production-safe output.

**Why that is better:** Buyers get a faster start and a clearer handoff. Printers, designers, and marketers can see what is safe, what needs review, and what the paid export includes.

**Why customers choose us:** They need more than a pretty image and less complexity than expert preflight software.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Canva already makes flyers and cards." | Canva is useful for design starts. Trim Proof is for the printer-facing handoff: PDF/X-oriented export, vector text, bleed, crop marks, color workflow, and preflight report. |
| "Acrobat or PitStop already checks PDFs." | Expert preflight tools inspect existing PDFs. Trim Proof starts earlier by generating a supported proof from a brief and making the print checks visible before paid export. |
| "Will my printer accept it?" | Printer specifications still control final acceptance. Trim Proof reduces avoidable structural issues and exposes the checks, but it does not guarantee every vendor's requirements. |
| "Why do I need an account for the demo?" | The account saves lead context, links future exports to the right Stripe customer, and lets saved designs and usage history work. |

**Anti-persona:** Users who need arbitrary existing-PDF repair, complex packaging dielines, enterprise approval routing, legal print compliance, or guaranteed printer acceptance without reviewing vendor specs.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Current tools create screen-ready output, require expert cleanup, or leave buyers guessing about print requirements.

**Pull:** A guided flow from brief to checked proof, with a free watermarked demo and paid clean export.

**Habit:** Buyers are used to Canva, Adobe, templates, or sending imperfect files to a printer and waiting for feedback.

**Anxiety:** They worry AI output will be sloppy, a printer will reject the file, or the product will not match vendor-specific specs.
