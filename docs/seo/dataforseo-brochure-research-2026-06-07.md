## Product Decision

Brochures should move from deferred opportunity to supported starter product because the highest-volume cluster now has a real Trim Proof conversion path:

- Template intent: `brochure template` and `brochure templates` at 18,100 monthly searches each.
- Tri-fold template intent: exact tri-fold/trifold variants at 4,400 monthly searches each.
- Maker intent: `brochure maker` and `brochure creator` at 1,900 each, with high-CPC AI/online variants.
- Setup intent: size, dimensions, panel, bleed, and pixel questions map to answer blocks and a checked proof workflow.

The implemented product boundary should stay narrow:

- Supported starter profile: 8.5 x 11 inch sheet in landscape orientation, represented as an 11 x 8.5 inch flat tri-fold proof.
- Bleed: 0.125 inch on every edge when artwork reaches trim.
- Safe margin: 0.25 inch plus fold-panel awareness for important text.
- Pixel math: 3300 x 2550 px at trim, 3375 x 2625 px with bleed at 300 DPI.
- Free/demo art is watermarked.
- Clean production PDF/X downloads require a paid export credit or Trim Proof Pro.
- Trim Proof does not sell printed brochures, folding, finishing, shipping, Word templates, or universal printer acceptance.
