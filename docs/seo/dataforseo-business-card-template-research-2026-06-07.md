## Page strategy

Expand `/tools/business-card-pdf-template` instead of adding a duplicate broad-template route.

The answer block should include:

- A good template must do more than look good on screen
- Standard US business-card trim: 3.5 x 2 inches
- Common bleed: 0.125 inch on each side
- Full-bleed document: 3.75 x 2.25 inches
- 300 DPI pixel dimensions: 1050 x 600 px trimmed, 1125 x 675 px full bleed
- Safe area for contact details
- Embedded vector text, crop marks when requested, color workflow, PDF/X-1a, and preflight
- Static-template and printer-acceptance caveats

The page should not claim that Trim Proof is a template marketplace, legal advisor, print vendor, free printing service, or guarantee of every printer's acceptance. It should state that Trim Proof generates a fresh checked PDF proof from a structured brief.
