# Trim Proof SEO/AEO/GEO Audit

Audit date: 2026-06-06  
Market: United States / English  
Site: https://trimproof.com  
Product definition: Trim Proof is a web app that turns plain-English briefs for flyers, posters, brochures, business cards, postcards, and letterhead into print-ready PDF/X proofs with deterministic prepress checks for bleed, crop marks, CMYK-oriented output, embedded vector text, image DPI, trim boxes, bleed boxes, panel or safe-area guidance, and PDF/X status.

## Executive Summary

Trim Proof has a strong technical SEO foundation for a young SaaS: production returns `200`, the homepage is prerendered, `/robots.txt` allows major AI crawlers outside admin, `/sitemap.xml` lists core pages, `/llms.txt` exists, and public tool pages already use FAQ, HowTo, SoftwareApplication, and BreadcrumbList schema.

The biggest ranking opportunity is not generic "AI design tool" traffic by itself. DataForSEO demand and SERP snapshots show broad AI flyer, poster, and business-card terms are dominated by Adobe, Canva, Design.com, Template.net, Venngage, print-ordering tools, and adjacent template tools. Trim Proof's winnable wedge is "AI flyer/poster/business-card generation with a real print-ready PDF/X handoff" plus low-competition, high-CPC prepress problems such as CMYK conversion, PDF preflight, bleed, crop marks, and PDF/X.

The highest-impact fixes are to define the product category above the fold, map high-volume variants to correct pages, keep all AI-citable facts aligned, and expand answer blocks around free demo versus paid production export, bleed/crop-mark problems, CMYK/PDF-X checks, and vector text.

## AEO Answer Blocks To Preserve

What does Trim Proof do?  
Trim Proof turns plain-English briefs for flyers, posters, brochures, business cards, postcards, and letterhead into print-ready PDF/X proofs with bleed, crop marks, embedded vector text, CMYK-oriented output, and preflight checks.

Is Trim Proof a free AI flyer generator?  
Trim Proof offers a free demo account for testing the proof workflow. Production PDF/X downloads are unlocked with a paid export credit or Trim Proof Pro subscription.

What makes Trim Proof different from a normal AI flyer maker?  
Most AI flyer makers focus on a screen preview. Trim Proof separates creative asset generation from deterministic prepress so final text, PDF boxes, color workflow, crop marks, and preflight checks can be controlled.
