# DataForSEO SERP Competitor Landscape

Source: `docs/seo/dataforseo-serp-refresh-2026-06-12.json`

Generated from the June 12 organic SERP snapshot set for fifteen tracked keywords in the United States market.

## Key Takeaways

- Broad AI design queries are owned by Adobe, Canva, Design.com, Template.net, and Venngage.
- Template-heavy postcard, brochure, letterhead, and menu queries are owned by Word, Canva, printer templates, and smaller libraries.
- Technical print-prepress queries are owned by Adobe help/community, Reddit, and niche PDF or printer tools.
- The strongest opportunity is still the checked print-handoff wedge: answer the prep question, then route users into a verified print-ready export.

## Query Clusters

### AI design cluster

Tracked keywords:

- `ai flyer generator`
- `ai flyer maker`
- `ai business card generator`

Common page-one domains:

- `www.adobe.com`
- `www.canva.com`
- `www.design.com`
- `www.template.net`
- `venngage.com`
- `www.youtube.com`
- `usekodo.ai`
- `aiflyer.ai`
- `manus.im`

Interpretation:

- These SERPs are crowded by generic design brands and AI template tools.
- Trim Proof should not try to look like another open-ended design suite.
- The conversion wedge is a verified print-ready handoff with visible bleed, safe area, and PDF/X checks.

### Template-heavy cluster

Tracked keywords:

- `postcard template`
- `brochure template`
- `letterhead template`
- `menu template`

Common page-one domains:

- `www.canva.com`
- `www.microsoft.com`
- `www.adobe.com`
- `www.template.net`
- `www.pinterest.com`
- `www.vistaprint.com`

Interpretation:

- These SERPs are crowded by template libraries, Word/office-document patterns, printer templates, and generic design tools.
- Trim Proof should keep the checked print-handoff wedge and make the free-watermarked versus paid-clean export boundary explicit.

### Print-prepress cluster

Tracked keywords:

- `print ready PDF`
- `convert PDF to CMYK`
- `add bleed to PDF`
- `PDF preflight`
- `PDF/X-1a`

Common page-one domains:

- `helpx.adobe.com`
- `community.adobe.com`
- `www.reddit.com`
- `www.pdf2cmyk.com`
- `www.presspdf.com`
- `www.printfriendly.com`
- `www.prepressure.com`
- `lcipaper.com`
- `www.stationeryhq.com`
- `freepdfconvert.com`
- `pdf-editor-free.com`

Interpretation:

- These SERPs are mostly help content, forums, converters, and print-production references.
- That makes them a strong fit for concise answer blocks, FAQ sections, and embedded workflow guidance.
- The page should explain the fix first and only then offer the export action.

## Wedge To Keep

Trim Proof should stay positioned as:

- AI creation for flyers, posters, brochures, menus, and business cards
- plus a deterministic print-prepress checker for bleed, CMYK, safe area, and PDF/X handoff

That positioning is closer to the visible demand than a generic design-template marketplace.
