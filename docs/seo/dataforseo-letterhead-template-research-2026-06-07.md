## Page strategy

Expand `/tools/letterhead-pdf-template` instead of adding a duplicate broad-template route.

The answer block should include:

- A good letterhead template must do more than open in Word or look good on screen
- US Letter starter profile: 8.5 x 11 inches
- A4 reference: 210 x 297 mm
- Optional bleed: 0.125 inch on every side when artwork reaches the edge
- Full-bleed US Letter document: 8.75 x 11.25 inches
- 300 DPI pixel dimensions: 2550 x 3300 px trimmed, 2625 x 3375 px full bleed
- Safe margins and writing space
- Embedded vector business details, PDF/X-1a, and preflight
- Static-template, Word-workflow, free-watermarked-demo, paid-clean-export, and printer-acceptance caveats

The page should not claim that Trim Proof is a template marketplace, Microsoft Word replacement, print vendor, free clean production download, or guarantee of every printer's acceptance. It should state that Trim Proof generates a fresh checked PDF proof from a structured brief.
