# DataForSEO Poster Research - 2026-06-07

## Summary

Poster moved from a deferred product cluster to a supported Trim Proof starter product after the app added an 11 x 17 inch poster profile. The research shows both large setup demand (`poster size`, `poster dimensions`) and maker/template demand (`poster maker`, `free poster maker`, `poster template`, `AI poster generator`). The conversion wedge is checked print handoff: watermarked free demo art, vector poster text, 11 x 17 trim, 0.125 inch bleed, 0.5 inch safe margin, PDF/X-1a preflight, and paid clean production downloads.

Trim Proof should not claim to sell printed posters, frames, shipping, stock, finishing, or print-shop services. Printer specifications still control final acceptance.

## SERP Shape

`poster maker` SERPs are dominated by Adobe Express, Canva, Google Play app results, Block Posters, Design.com, PosterMyWall, MYPOSTER, Reddit, Vistaprint, Microsoft Designer, Pinterest, and Venngage. These competitors mostly offer design templates, app-store tools, print ordering, or social graphics. Trim Proof should differentiate around the checked PDF/X handoff instead of competing as a generic template app.

`poster size` SERPs are dominated by frame, printer, Canva, university PDF, Printivity, Reddit, Shutterstock, 48HourPrint, YouTube, and Solopress pages. People Also Ask questions include whether 24 x 36 is standard, what a standard poster size is, whether 18 x 24 is a poster size, and whether 11 x 17 is poster size. Trim Proof should answer the sizing question first and then route to the proof workflow.

## Content Boundaries

- Free demo art is watermarked.
- Clean production PDF/X-1a downloads require a paid export credit or Trim Proof Pro.
- Trim Proof creates checked poster PDF proofs and production downloads.
- Trim Proof does not sell printed posters, frames, paper, finishing, shipping, mailing, or print-shop services.
- Trim Proof does not guarantee printer acceptance.
- The supported starter poster profile is 11 x 17 inches with 0.125 inch bleed and a 0.5 inch safe margin.
