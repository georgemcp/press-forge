## Page strategy

Expand `/tools/flyer-pdf-template` instead of adding a duplicate broad-template route.

The answer block should include:

- A good template must do more than look good on screen
- Supported starter flyer trim: 8.5 x 11 inches
- Common bleed: 0.125 inch on each side
- Full-bleed document: 8.75 x 11.25 inches
- 300 DPI pixel dimensions: 2550 x 3300 px trimmed, 2625 x 3375 px full bleed
- Safe area for headlines, offers, dates, QR codes, disclaimers, and contact details
- Embedded vector text, crop marks when requested, color workflow, PDF/X-1a, and preflight
- Static-template and printer-acceptance caveats

The page should not claim that Trim Proof is a template marketplace, print vendor, free production download, or guarantee of every printer's acceptance. It should state that Trim Proof generates a fresh checked PDF proof from a structured brief and that free demo art is watermarked while clean production exports are paid.
